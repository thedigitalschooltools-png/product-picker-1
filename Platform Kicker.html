📌 Data kahan se aaya (Oct 2026 tak research)
  • Amazon: 16 Mar 2026 se ₹1,000 se kam ke 12.5 crore+ products (1,800+ categories) par referral fee ₹0; closing fee ₹20–26 (7 Sep 2026 se ₹1–3 badha), weight handling alag.
  • Flipkart: ₹1,000 se kam par zero commission (Nov 2025); sab fashion par zero commission (8 Jul 2026). Baaki me fixed fee + shipping + ~2% collection fee, upar se 18% GST.
  • Meesho: 0% commission; sirf shipping (weight/zone ke hisaab se) + GST. Returns ka asar bada hota hai.
  • Own website: Gateway 2% + GST (≈2.36%), Shopify India me extra 2%, courier ₹40–120/500g, RTO 15–30%, aur ads/CAC.
⚠️ Rates indicative hain — sub-category, seller tier aur zone se badalte hain. Final decision se pehle apne Seller Hub / Seller Central ka rate card check karo. Product par lagne wala GST yahan include nahi hai.
